# Code of Conduct

**as of 2026-09-19**

## The short version

Argue with the claim, not the person. Technical disagreement is the point of this repository; contempt is not.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages and review comments in this repository — anywhere the project touches other people.

## Expected

- **Attack the claim.** "This heuristic mis-fires on `except X as e: raise` — here is the file" is welcome and useful. "Whoever wrote this rule is careless" is neither.
- **Assume a reproduction is offered in good faith**, even when it turns out not to reproduce. An unreproducible report is still someone telling you where they looked.
- **Say when you are unsure.** "I think this is a false positive but I could not minimise it" is a valid report, and the uncertainty is information.
- **Respect the de-identification rules.** They exist to protect people who are not in this conversation. A report that names a client or an employer's internals will be closed, not quietly edited.
- **Accept a "no" with a reason.** Several categories are out of scope by design — see [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md) § An entry is rejected when. A rejection that cites the rule is not a judgement on you.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or sustained personal criticism.
- Discriminatory or sexualised remarks of any kind.
- Publishing anyone's private information, including as part of a "reproduction".
- **Pasting credentials, tokens or customer data** — see [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md). Posting a live secret harms a third party who never agreed to be in this thread.
- **Any attempt to gain stars, forks or engagement dishonestly** on this repository — coordinated starring, purchased stars, or offers to trade them. This is prohibited by the platform and it is the failure this catalog documents, applied to itself.
- **Using an issue thread to advertise a product, a service or yourself.** If your work is genuinely relevant, say why in your own words and let the reader decide.
- Sustained disruption: reopening a decided question without new information, or restating a position after it has been answered.

## Reporting a problem

Report conduct problems through **GitHub's private vulnerability reporting channel** — *Security* tab → *Report a vulnerability* — even though it is not a security issue. It is the only private channel this repository has, and using it keeps the report off the public timeline while it is considered.

There is no email address and no committee. This is a personal project, maintained by one person, on a best-effort basis.

## Enforcement

| Response | When |
|---|---|
| A comment naming the rule | First instance, minor — usually enough |
| A locked thread | The disagreement has run its course and is now generating heat, not information |
| A block, and removal of the content | Harassment, doxxing, or a repeated violation |
| A permanent ban | Threats, or anything illegal |

Enforcement is at the maintainer's discretion. Where a decision is contestable, the reason will be stated in public so it can be argued with — a moderation action that leaves no stated reason is the same failure mode this catalog is about.

## On this document

This is not a verbatim adoption of any standard code of conduct, and it does not claim to be. It is short on purpose: a code nobody reads governs nothing, and a repository whose subject is *unverifiable claims* should not open with one.
